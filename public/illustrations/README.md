# Dictionary illustrations

## Abscissa

`abscissa.png` is the coordinate diagram from _Webster's International Dictionary_ (1907), printed page 7, [Internet Archive scan n112](https://archive.org/download/webstersinternat01webs/page/n112.jpg?scale=1).

The 156 × 129 pixel image is an unchanged crop of the 2399 × 3371 pixel scan, bounded by (646, 1577) and (802, 1706). O is the origin. X and Y mark the horizontal and vertical projections of P.

## Alternate angles

`alternate-angles.png` is the diagram accompanying _Alternate angles_ in _Webster's International Dictionary_ (1907), printed page 44, [Internet Archive scan n149](https://archive.org/download/webstersinternat01webs/page/n149.jpg?scale=1).

The 300 × 262 pixel image is an unchanged crop of the 2399 × 3371 pixel scan, bounded by (2040, 271) and (2340, 533). Parallel lines AB and CD are cut by transversal EF at G and H.

## Arcade

`arcade.png` is the engraving accompanying _Arcade_ in _Webster's International Dictionary_ (1907), printed page 77, [Internet Archive scan n182](https://archive.org/download/webstersinternat01webs/page/n182.jpg?scale=1).

The 380 × 295 pixel image is an unchanged crop of the 2399 × 3371 pixel scan, bounded by (1810, 1120) and (2190, 1415). Pointed arches rest on columns in front of a closing wall.

## Ampersand

`ampersand-form-one.png` and `ampersand-form-two.png` are the two typographic forms preceding the ordinary ampersand in _Webster’s International Dictionary_ (1907), printed page 50, [Internet Archive scan n155](https://archive.org/download/webstersinternat01webs/page/n155.jpg?scale=1).

The unchanged crops of the 2399 × 3371 pixel scan are bounded by (571, 1035) and (593, 1059), and by (610, 1035) and (633, 1059), respectively.

## Appoggiatura

`appoggiatura.png` is the musical example accompanying _Appoggiatura_ in _Webster’s International Dictionary_ (1907), printed page 73, [Internet Archive scan n178](https://archive.org/download/webstersinternat01webs/page/n178.jpg?scale=1).

The unchanged 170 × 112 pixel crop of the 2399 × 3371 pixel scan is bounded by (613, 2260) and (783, 2372). Both grace-note and chord examples are preserved.
