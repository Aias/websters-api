# Page-reading batch 4

Two models read printed pages 151 to 250 without seeing the record, and their agreed reading is compared with the record word by word. This batch makes 877 corrections in 629 entries, adds 14 entries that the electronic text never had, and gives 16 records their printed spelling. It opens eleven holds, each of them a question in the review queue. The batch grants no complete-entry review credit.

The method is the one [batch 2](dictionary-audit-page-reading-2.md) describes, with the changes [batch 3](dictionary-audit-page-reading-3.md) made. This report gives what differs.

## Method changes

- **Pages:** leaves n256 to n355 of the 1907 volume 1 scan, Black book through Chogset, about 251,000 words in 6,284 printed entries.
- **Second reader:** Fable read 202 of the 300 columns and Sonnet read the other 98. The two readings differ in 137 places, 0.55 per thousand words.
- **Rewritten definitions:** where GCIDE rewrote a definition and part of the new wording is a registered later addition, replacing the differing words one at a time leaves a sentence that is neither text. Three entries came out that way (Bunn, Capelin and Carcinoma) and were set back to their earlier text. Such a definition now goes to the review queue as one question, and every record a batch touches is read whole, before and after, for a sentence of this kind.
- **Earlier batch:** the same scan of batches 2 and 3 found one stray space, in Anorexia, which this batch removes.

## Corrections

- **784 follow the ruling of 10 November 2026.** The record's reading came from the typists. Examples: Boy "Christmas" for "Christian", Bunkum "popularity" for "popularly", Burro "Southwestern" for "Southern", Capacify "benefits" for "benefice", Chalk "black color" for "color", Cates's authority "Churchill" for "Shurchill", Capricorn's family "Cerambycidæ" for "Carambucidæ", and plain typing slips such as "cristallization", "buidings", "Rebublican" and "arictocrats". Charlotte regains "sliced apples, sugar, butter, etc., covered with", Candle regains "wax, spermaceti, paraffine, or some similar substance", and Bode regains the etymology clause "message, fr. the root of beódan to command".
- **93 undo a later GCIDE change.** Examples: Caaba and Cadi "Mohammedan" for "Muslim", Canada "British province" for "country", Bottling "cork" for "seal", Camel "bunch" for "hump", Bluntness "want" for "lack", Cation "An electro-positive substance" and Catalysis "soluble ferment" for "enzyme". Burner regains its printed account of the Bunsen burner, and Buffalo, Burn, Chamber, Charcoal, Chimney and China regain phrases GCIDE had moved or dropped.
- **Bossy** regains the quotation the print sets under the adjective, "His head reclining on his bossy shield. Pope."
- **Cacajao** gives the species as the print does, "Pithecia (or Brachyurus) melanocephala".
- **Cart** prints its heading as the page does, "To put (or get or set) the cart before the horse", with the three complete names kept as annotations.
- **Canonship** had the definition of the next printed entry. It now reads "Canonry."
- **Causeless** and **Ceaseless** each carried an adverb sense that the print sets as the entries Causelessly and Ceaselessly. Those are now their own records.

Usage marks gain what the print sets in 4 entries, and 12 definitions regain the authority printed after them.

## Kept and held

- **The print is wrong:** Centrifugal drops a letter where "different" breaks across two lines.
- **A convention keeps the record:** Bolus and Chastise write "Gr." where the print sets "G." before a Greek word. Carbamic writes its formula in the record's notation.
- **The 1898 printing reads as the record (13):** worn or broken type in the 1907 impression, such as Candy "erystals" and Casual "oelong".
- **Held (11 questions):** nine definitions that GCIDE rewrote, where restoring the printed wording removes text the later-additions register lists (Bluing, Bunn, Camphor, Capelin, Carbureted, Carcinoma, Cardinal, Catsup and Chaetognatha). Bossy's etymology, which differs from the printed "[Cf. Bos.]" and which no register lists. Centipede, which the print sets under the headword Centiped. Each is in the review queue with crops of both printings.

## Limits

- Pronunciation marks, punctuation, capitals and italics are not compared.
- In the 98 columns Sonnet read, about 8 words in 10,000 go uncompared where it misreads.
- An entry that runs over from page 150 or onto page 251 is compared only as far as this batch's pages.
- The 1913 printing itself was not read. The only scan found is access-restricted.
