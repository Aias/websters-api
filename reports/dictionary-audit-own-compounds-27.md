# Own-record compound dispositions, batch 27

This batch reviews the last 43 candidates of the 188-key own-record compound pool. Each printed compound is first found in the two blind page readings of its column, and the head record's unit is compared with both readings word by word. A compound the head record lacks is then read again by two blind Opus readers from enlarged crops of two printings, who each write the exact record fragment. Every standalone record stays byte-identical. The batch grants no complete-entry credit.

## Sub-batch 1: printed compounds missing from their heads

Eleven candidates are resolved. Ten printed units return to nine head entries: Telegraph plant under Telegraph, Try cock under Try, Deaf and dumb under Deaf, Knock off under Knock, Caddice fly under Caddice, Cæsarean section under Cæsarean, Manila hemp and Manila paper under Manila, Olympic games under Olympic, and the pointer "Valued policy. See under Policy." under Valued. Ray already prints X ray as the record has it, and its neighbouring compound Röntgen ray regains its printed definition, "a kind of ray generated in a very highly exhausted vacuum tube by the electrical discharge", in place of GCIDE's rewrite about electromagnetic radiation.

The [ledger](dictionary-audit-own-compounds-27.jsonl) records ten source edits. Eight are independent agreements of two certain readers. Two are source adjudications. One Manila reader was unsure of a smudged letter in "Musa", and both Röntgen readers were unsure of letters the 1898 crop cut off at the column edge. Two fresh certain readers of better crops confirmed each exact fragment, and Gutenberg's 1913 text agrees word for word. The 1898 printing spells the Manila compounds "Manilla" and arranges the Manila entries differently. The record follows the later printing, which Gutenberg also follows.

The later-additions register retires the three Ray spans that the restored definition replaces. Knock-off becomes a 1913 Supplement entry, because its two GCIDE paragraphs and Gutenberg's supplement paragraphs 4328 and 4329 match it and the PJC sense its old row counted is absent. Knock off loses its entry row, because it holds only the printed Knock compound. Deaf-and-dumb records its GCIDE editor tag. Two printed figures on the Caddice page join the [illustration register](dictionary-audit-illustrations.jsonl).

Knock off now repeats the restored compound word for word. The duplicate ruling removes such records, but removal also drops the record's own route, so the [holds register](dictionary-audit-holds.jsonl) asks the person whether to remove it.

The lookup comparison adds one route: "cæsarean_section" now reaches the printed compound under Cæsarian. The ligature spelling had reached the modern own record through equivalence, and the record's own key still does. Nine compound names gain a second claimant, and each still resolves to its existing canonical record.

Six blind reader calls settled this sub-batch: four initial readings of five targets each, and two fresh readings of the two adjudicated targets.

## Sub-batch 2: compounds the head records already carry

The other 32 candidates are resolved without a crop reading of their compound. For 26 of them the head record already carries the printed compound, and its words match both page readings of the column. The remaining differences are cuts at a unit's edge, spelled-out labels and genera, the registered later additions under White elephant and Atlas powder, and one reader's slip in Valued policy. Each own record stays: most are 1913 Supplement entries, and the rest carry later wording. Prickly ash is a compound of Prickly only, because Ash names it inside a definition. Tungsten steel's compound runs across a column break.

Six candidates were tied to a head by mistake. K, Year and Yellow are themselves printed main entries. The print sets Kerseys and Rallies only as plurals of Kersey and Rally, and Pinto only as the singular of the people Pintos, so those three own records are Supplement entries with no printed compound to restore.

Bourbon whisky and Garpike are printed main entries ("See under Whisky", "See under Gar"), and Year matches both page readings word for word, so their later-addition rows go. Yellow's row gives way to span rows for its two Supplement senses, "Cowardly" and "Sensational", from Gutenberg's Supplement paragraphs 7660 and 7661. Telephone exchange records its GCIDE editor tag for the later sentence on telephone lines. These settle the last four classification-review keys the pool shared: Lightweight and Snap shot keep their classifications.

Reading Yellow against the page found that GCIDE had rewritten the anatomical sense of Yellow spot. It takes back the printed definition, "A small yellowish depression in the center of the retina where vision is most acute; — called also fovea centralis. See Illust. of Eye." Two certain blind readers agree on every word. They differ only in the spacing around the dash and in the markup of "Illust.", and the fragment takes the corpus forms, which appear 2,115 and 376 times against none for theirs.

Worthwhile repeats the printed compound under While word for word, like Knock off, and joins the same duplicate-record question.

Two blind reader calls settled this sub-batch.

## Totals

All 43 candidates have dispositions, and the 188-key pool has no candidate left. Eleven source edits restore ten compound units and two printed definitions: nine independent agreements and two source adjudications. No proposal was rejected. Two duplicate-record questions join the holds register, and two printed figures join the illustration register. Eight blind reader calls were made in all.
