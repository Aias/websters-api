# Page-reading batch 17

Two models read printed pages 1461 to 1560 without seeing the record, and their agreed reading is compared with the record word by word. This batch makes 713 corrections in 544 entries, adds 2 entries that had no record of their own, and gives 12 records their printed spelling. It opens 20 holds: 19 questions in the review queue and one difference in the order of same-spelled entries. The batch grants no complete-entry review credit.

The method is the one [batch 2](dictionary-audit-page-reading-2.md) describes, with the changes of batches [3](dictionary-audit-page-reading-3.md) to [12](dictionary-audit-page-reading-12.md). Nothing in the method changed for this batch.

## Pages and readers

- **Pages:** leaves n508 to n607 of the 1895 volume 2 scan, Sycock through Ultimatum, about 249,000 words in 6,250 printed entries.
- **Readers:** Opus and Sonnet. Opus alone found 62 differences the pair had missed, Fable read those strips blind and agreed on 36, and 7 became corrections. A second, taller strip of the 1898 printing settled 29 of 42 differences that the first strip had left unclear.
- **Proofreading:** four reviewers read all 573 rule-drafted corrections and reported 77. Thirty-three of the reports are single words where the 1913 typists' text agrees with the record and both readings make sense. Under the ruling of 10 November 2026 those follow the print, and the ledger lists them. Reports that a correction would mix the printed wording with a later rewrite went to the person.

## Corrections

- **673 follow the ruling of 10 November 2026.** The record's reading came from the typists. Examples: Toss "cars" for "oars", Teapoy "there" for "three", Toilet "snake" for "make", Thistly "pricking" for "prickling", Toleration "judgment" for "judging", and the field label "(Mus.)" for "(Mil.)" on the soldiers' punishment frame under Triangle. Term regains the phrase "Terms of ratio", Take "To take the ground", Tack "the latter when closehauled with the wind on her port side", Time "time reckoned for all places from some one common epoch", and Threadbare "trite; hackneyed; without novelty or interest". Thorpe, Thursday, Translate and Trinket regain words dropped from their etymologies.
- **40 undo a later GCIDE change.** Take regains the phrase "To take off" with its eleven senses. Torus again reads "See 3d Tore, 2", Technique "Same as Technic, n.", and Tunicata "by some writers united with the latter". Tobacco, Trionyx, Turban-top and Turnip regain the printed form of a name with two genera. Trinitarian and Turn regain "Mohammedans" and "Mohammedan", Tubercle "exciting" for "causative", and Trypsin "ferment, or enzyme" and "proteid matter".
- **Inflected forms:** 11 are corrected, among them Tenancy "Tenancies", Thunderstrike "-stricken", Tipstaff "Tipstaves", Topman "Topmen" and Tranquillize "Tranquillized".
- **Teste and Testify** each carried a neighbouring printed entry under their own headword. Tested and Testily are now records of their own.
- **Small slips fixed by hand:** Thin regains "thin-soled", Trade the first line of its Dryden couplet, Trash "to trash trees", and Transfigurate its authority "Byron.". Tourist again "performs a journey in a circuit", Trigenic reads "ethidene- or ethylidene-biuret", Teak's phrase is "New Zealand teak", Thirst's Greek word regains a lost letter, and Top, Translate and Token have their abbreviations and capitals as printed.
- **One correction lies outside these pages.** Rule, on page 1259, read "it is a rule in England" where both readers and the 1898 printing have "in English". [Batch 14](dictionary-audit-page-reading-14.md) had set the difference aside because the words before it repeat the end of a registered later addition. A proofreader of the punctuation pass noticed it.

## Kept and held

- **The print is wrong (8):** broken or missing letters give "tne" under Table-land and Toll, "aud" under Therapeutical, "fcr" under Term, "tbe" under Tichorrhine, "sufciently" under Timely and "conected" under Transit. Tristoma has "parasite" for "parasitic".
- **Probable 1913 revisions:** Syenite, Sympodium, Teleutospore, Tellurous, Thrusting, Time, Toluric, Tree and Trinket stand as the 1913 typists' text has them. Under Time the older printings give the pace of quick time as it was before the drill regulations of 1891. Tannage keeps Browning's "his cheek fresh tannage", which the older printings misquote.
- **The record follows the 1913 Supplement:** Sylvanite, Tactical, Tan, Thermocurrent, Tramway, Trust, Try and Ulema.
- **Held for the person (19 questions):** 18 are passages that GCIDE rewrote, where restoring the printed wording removes text the later-additions register lists or would blend two wordings: Tableware, Tacky, Tantrum, Tap, Teaspoonful, Terbium, Thermostat, Thread, Thulium, Thyroid, Tiger, Tillandsia, Tire, Tote, Tunny, Turacou, Turbid and Ulterior. The nineteenth is a quotation under Transfigurate, described below. Each is in the review queue with crops of both printings.
- **Held for a later pass (1):** Thou, whose same-spelled entries stand in a different order from the print.

## A difference between the two printings

Under Transfigurate the volume 2 scan prints "[R.]" followed by a quotation, "High heaven is there, transfused, transfigurated.", and the authority Byron. The 1898 printing has "[R.]" and the authority alone. The quotation was therefore added to the plates after the 1898 copy was printed, which means this scan is of a later impression than its catalogue date of 1895 suggests. The record had neither. It now has the authority, which both printings support, and the quotation is a question for the person.

That is about 367,000 tokens a page and 5,900 an entry. The integrating session's own usage was not measured. The tiebreak reading ran twice, because its first run fell in a usage outage and returned nothing.

## Limits

- Pronunciation marks, punctuation, capitals and italics are not compared here. The [punctuation and capitals pass](dictionary-audit-punctuation.md) covers two of those.
- A single word that the 1913 printing changed cannot be told from a typist's slip without the 1913 page. The ruling of 10 November 2026 treats it as a slip.
- An entry that runs onto page 1561 is compared only as far as this batch's pages.
- The 1913 printing itself was not read. The only scan found is access-restricted.
