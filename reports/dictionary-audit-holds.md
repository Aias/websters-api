# Open dictionary audit holds

[`dictionary-audit-holds.jsonl`](dictionary-audit-holds.jsonl) records 216 unresolved fields. 145 come from the earlier audit: 90 from the ascending audit through Metaleptic and from earlier reversed exceptions, 17 from descending batches z10 through z23, Synchysis back to Sore, 37 lost-character runs that no earlier hold tracked, and 1 from the stress-notation pass. The other twelve come from the batches that reconcile the text with the 1913 print. These include Dolphin's nested-sense note ownership. The [lost-character restoration of November 2026](dictionary-audit-glyphs-20261101.md) adds 59: 4 markers no reading has settled, 2 printed signs with no exact Unicode character, and 53 passages no reader found in any printing's crop. Batch z24 restored 6 of its original 198 held rows from the print, a second reading against two later printings restored 132, and batch z25 restored 1. Each row carries the batch, key, field, available print pages, and the reviewer's reason. Rows held at the decision stage also carry the exact `old` and `new` fragments with the frozen raw hash. Those fragments predate the dictionary-wide passes in 294127f through 37f9a35, so re-derive them from current source before use.

The ascending audit has examined 1012 entries. Of these, 527 are closed and 485 retain at least one field hold. Completed source, parser, browser-text, and visual reviews remain valid for their recorded inputs while the named field stays unresolved. Bare unknown pronunciations are intentional omissions under the source rules and are excluded from this list.

The editorial rulings in the dictionary-audit skill's [sources reference](../.agents/skills/dictionary-audit/references/sources-and-lexical-behavior.md#editorial-rulings) settled the questions these rows used to wait on. Applying them closed 142 rows without a source edit, a rulings pass of two independent readings per field has since corrected 66 more, a breathing pass restored 414 Greek words, and a cleanup round of the same readings restored 263 more fields. [`dictionary-audit-resolved-holds.jsonl`](dictionary-audit-resolved-holds.jsonl) lists all 1032 with the rule or batch that resolved them. These include 29 rows settled by the earlier whole-definition and sense batches and 33 settled by [definition-5](dictionary-audit-definition-5.md). Definition-5 resolves those fields without adding whole-entry review credit. Earlier dispositions include:

- 77 rows kept the electronic text under a ruling, and Sycophancy's "A. Philips" was fixed by the initial-space pass.
- 64 rows were printed figures that the electronic text lacks. They moved to [`dictionary-audit-illustrations.jsonl`](dictionary-audit-illustrations.jsonl) for a later illustrations pass, which also lists figures that new batches find.

Most of the remaining `lsj-greek` and `untracked-lost-characters` rows sit in entries that no available printing settles. Some, such as Actinogram and Telegraphone, are absent from both the scanned 1895 and 1907 main volumes and the 1900 supplement in HathiTrust's 1907 Merriam printing (item uiug.30112099825041), so they wait on a printing with the enlarged supplement of about 1909 to 1913. Others print fewer Greek words than the record lost, as with Ascomycetes and Anisometropia. Each row's reason says where the cleanup round looked.

Shared parsing and source-boundary issues, including Make's compound separator, Meditate's synonym scope, and the Metabola homograph link, are recorded in [`dictionary-structural-issues.jsonl`](dictionary-structural-issues.jsonl).

Earth's proposed provenance hold is superseded before publication by the complete Gutenberg supplement witness. The two Lathe fields add two reading holds to the earlier reading groups.
