# Open dictionary audit holds

[`dictionary-audit-holds.jsonl`](dictionary-audit-holds.jsonl) records 737 unresolved fields: 641 from the ascending audit through Metaleptic and from earlier reversed exceptions, and 96 from descending batches z10 through z22, Synchysis back to Sparth. Each row carries the batch, key, field, available print pages, and the reviewer's reason. Rows held at the decision stage also carry the exact `old` and `new` fragments with the frozen raw hash. Those fragments predate the dictionary-wide passes in 294127f through 37f9a35, so re-derive them from current source before use.

The ascending audit has examined 1012 entries. Of these, 527 are closed and 485 retain at least one field hold. Completed source, parser, browser-text, and visual reviews remain valid for their recorded inputs while the named field stays unresolved. Bare unknown pronunciations are intentional omissions under the source rules and are excluded from this list.

The editorial rulings in the dictionary-audit skill's [sources reference](../.agents/skills/dictionary-audit/references/sources-and-lexical-behavior.md#editorial-rulings) settled the questions these rows used to wait on. Applying them closed 142 rows without a source edit:

- 77 rows kept the electronic text under a ruling. They are listed with the rule in [`dictionary-audit-resolved-holds.jsonl`](dictionary-audit-resolved-holds.jsonl), together with Sycophancy's "A. Philips", which the initial-space pass fixed.
- 64 rows were printed figures that the electronic text lacks. They moved to [`dictionary-audit-illustrations.jsonl`](dictionary-audit-illustrations.jsonl) for a later illustrations pass, which also lists figures that new batches find.
