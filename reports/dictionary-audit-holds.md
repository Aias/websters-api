# Open dictionary audit holds

[`dictionary-audit-holds.jsonl`](dictionary-audit-holds.jsonl) records 249 unresolved fields: 138 from the ascending audit through Metaleptic and from earlier reversed exceptions, 20 from descending batches z10 through z23, Synchysis back to Sore, 90 lost-character runs that no earlier hold tracked, and 1 from the stress-notation pass. Each row carries the batch, key, field, available print pages, and the reviewer's reason. Rows held at the decision stage also carry the exact `old` and `new` fragments with the frozen raw hash. Those fragments predate the dictionary-wide passes in 294127f through 37f9a35, so re-derive them from current source before use.

The ascending audit has examined 1012 entries. Of these, 527 are closed and 485 retain at least one field hold. Completed source, parser, browser-text, and visual reviews remain valid for their recorded inputs while the named field stays unresolved. Bare unknown pronunciations are intentional omissions under the source rules and are excluded from this list.

The editorial rulings in the dictionary-audit skill's [sources reference](../.agents/skills/dictionary-audit/references/sources-and-lexical-behavior.md#editorial-rulings) settled the questions these rows used to wait on. Applying them closed 142 rows without a source edit, a rulings pass of two independent readings per field has since corrected 66 more, a breathing pass restored 414 Greek words, and a cleanup round of the same readings restored 162 more fields. [`dictionary-audit-resolved-holds.jsonl`](dictionary-audit-resolved-holds.jsonl) lists all 723 with the rule or commit that resolved them:

- 77 rows kept the electronic text under a ruling, and Sycophancy's "A. Philips" was fixed by the initial-space pass.
- 64 rows were printed figures that the electronic text lacks. They moved to [`dictionary-audit-illustrations.jsonl`](dictionary-audit-illustrations.jsonl) for a later illustrations pass, which also lists figures that new batches find.

A row's `needs` value says what would resolve it:

Most of the remaining `lsj-greek` and `untracked-lost-characters` rows sit in entries that the scanned 1895 and 1907 printings lack, such as Actinogram and Appendicectomy. They wait on a printing that carries the 1900 supplement, and each row's reason says where the cleanup round looked.

Shared parsing and source-boundary issues, including Make's compound separator, Meditate's synonym scope, and the Metabola homograph link, are recorded in [`dictionary-structural-issues.jsonl`](dictionary-structural-issues.jsonl).
