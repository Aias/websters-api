# Open dictionary audit holds

[`dictionary-audit-holds.jsonl`](dictionary-audit-holds.jsonl) records 226 unresolved fields. 144 come from the earlier audit: 89 from the ascending audit through Metaleptic and from earlier reversed exceptions, 17 from descending batches z10 through z23, Synchysis back to Sore, 37 lost-character runs that no earlier hold tracked, and 1 from the stress-notation pass. Own-compounds-21-26 adds seven source questions without complete-entry credit. Seven more come from the earlier batches that reconcile the text with the 1913 print, including Dolphin's nested-sense note ownership. Descending batches z26 through z30, Sleight back to Scomm, add 6. Batches z24 and z25 left none. The quick-wins pass over Directoire style through Extensibleness left 31, and two blind readings of the 1895 print then settled 26 of them. Three printed readings differ from the proposed fix (Dislike's "against", Distillation's "coal tar from coal", Embryonated's "an embryo; embryonal"), and Element's later rewritten sentence gives way to the printed one. Earth's electrical sense is not printed, so its row waits on a 1913 supplement witness, and Exchange's four corrections wait on the own-record compound batch. The [lost-character restoration of November 2026](dictionary-audit-glyphs-20261101.md) adds 57: 4 markers no reading has settled and 53 passages no reader found in any printing's crop. Batch z24 restored 6 of its original 198 held rows from the print, a second reading against two later printings restored 132, batch z25 restored 1, and a ruling settled the 2 printed signs with no exact Unicode character. Each row carries the batch, key, field, available print pages, and the reviewer's reason. Rows held at the decision stage also carry the exact `old` and `new` fragments with the frozen raw hash. Those fragments predate the dictionary-wide passes in 294127f through 37f9a35, so re-derive them from current source before use.

The ascending audit has examined 1012 entries, -ite through Metaleptic. Of these, 927 have no open hold and 85 retain at least one field hold. The descending audit has examined 1179 entries, Xanthomelanous back through Scomm. Of these, 1149 have no open hold and 30 retain at least one. Completed source, parser, browser-text, and visual reviews remain valid for their recorded inputs while the named field stays unresolved. Bare unknown pronunciations are intentional omissions under the source rules and are excluded from this list.

The editorial rulings in the dictionary-audit skill's [sources reference](../.agents/skills/dictionary-audit/references/sources-and-lexical-behavior.md#editorial-rulings) settled the questions these rows used to wait on. Applying them closed 142 rows without a source edit, a rulings pass of two independent readings per field has since corrected 66 more, a breathing pass restored 414 Greek words, and a cleanup round of the same readings restored 263 more fields. [`dictionary-audit-resolved-holds.jsonl`](dictionary-audit-resolved-holds.jsonl) lists all 1070 with the rule or batch that resolved them. Rulings of November 2 closed the latest ten: Lancet, Siren and Sepiostare keep their electronic wording without the print-only article, Shab keeps its printed citation, Severe, Seborrhea and Scruple take the corrected readings, and Pantile and Turn take the nearest Unicode characters. The voiced th encoding, `t&#x35F;h`, then closed both Lathe rows. These include 29 rows settled by the earlier whole-definition and sense batches and 33 settled by [definition-5](dictionary-audit-definition-5.md). Definition-5 resolves those fields without adding whole-entry review credit. Earlier dispositions include:

- 77 rows kept the electronic text under a ruling, and Sycophancy's "A. Philips" was fixed by the initial-space pass.
- 64 rows were printed figures that the electronic text lacks. They moved to [`dictionary-audit-illustrations.jsonl`](dictionary-audit-illustrations.jsonl) for a later illustrations pass, which also lists figures that new batches find.

Most of the remaining `lsj-greek` and `untracked-lost-characters` rows sit in entries that no available printing settles. Some, such as Actinogram and Telegraphone, are absent from both the scanned 1895 and 1907 main volumes and the 1900 supplement in HathiTrust's 1907 Merriam printing (item uiug.30112099825041), so they wait on a printing with the enlarged supplement of about 1909 to 1913. A HathiTrust full-text search in November 2026 found no full-view Merriam printing with that supplement. The only full-view volume containing Actinogram, Telegraphone and Seismogram is the 1914 Century Dictionary, so these rows stay held until a scan of that printing becomes available. Others print fewer Greek words than the record lost, as with Ascomycetes and Anisometropia. Each row's reason says where the cleanup round looked.
