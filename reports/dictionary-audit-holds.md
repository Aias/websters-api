1. **Where GCIDE rewrote a printed definition, sense or etymology and the 1913 typists' text confirms the print, does the printed wording return as a unit even where later-addition rows mark the rewrite or a different whole sentence stands in its place?** 349 rows. Examples: Abrasion, Battalion, Betulin, Chincona. Options: restore the printed wording as a unit and retire the register rows that marked the rewrite; keep GCIDE's wording and its rows; or decide passage by passage. Each row quotes both wordings. [`dictionary-audit-rewrite-proposals.jsonl`](dictionary-audit-rewrite-proposals.jsonl) prepares each of them: 320 rest on two blind page readings and apply cleanly, 1 needs one more reading, 16 differ from both readings and need their source confirmed, and 12 have no drafted replacement. Once the ruling is given, apply each `ready` row by replacing its `old` with `new` in the record whose SHA-256 is `recordOldSha256` (refusing any record that has changed since), retire or move the register rows its `register` list names in the same change, close the hold, and run `bun test`.
2. **Do two agreeing closer readings, one of them Fable, overrule a mark (a tick, a line under n, a bar, a dot count) that only a page reader saw?** 132 rows. 118 headword pronunciations and 14 form pronunciations, held in the pronunciation ledgers rather than this register (examples: Intercrural, Ironmaster, Juncite). Options: take the closer readings; take the page reader's mark; or keep them held for a further reading.

Options: keep the later numbering, or set the printed sense unnumbered and the later senses after it; for the second kind, restore the printed senses or keep the record.

7. **Where GCIDE moved printed words to another place in the same entry and changed no word, does the printed order return?** 12 rows. Examples: Force, Index, Long. Options: restore the printed order, retiring any register row on the moved words; or keep the record's order.
8. **Does a later-added entry keep the ‖ (foreign-word mark) that its record carries?** 10 rows. Examples: Homeless, Intern, Jawbone. Options: remove the ‖ from later-added entries; or keep it.
