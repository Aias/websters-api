# Page-reading batch 10

Two models read printed pages 751 to 850 without seeing the record, and their agreed reading is compared with the record word by word. This batch makes 798 corrections in 631 entries, adds 12 entries that the electronic text never had, and gives 12 records their printed spelling. It opens 56 holds: 54 questions in the review queue and 2 entries whose homographs stand in another order than the print's. The batch grants no complete-entry review credit.

The method is the one [batch 2](dictionary-audit-page-reading-2.md) describes, with the changes of batches [3](dictionary-audit-page-reading-3.md) to [7](dictionary-audit-page-reading-7.md). One thing changed in this batch. A correction that a proofreader finds would mix the printed wording with a later rewrite now goes to the person as a question. [Batch 9](dictionary-audit-page-reading-9.md) had kept four such differences without one (Groschen, Gunner, Homoiothermic and Horsehair), and those are questions now too.

## Pages and readers

- **Pages:** leaves n856 to n955 of the 1907 volume 1 scan, Independent through Lifeless, about 246,000 words in 6,884 printed entries.
- **Readers:** Opus and Sonnet. Opus alone found 50 differences the pair had missed, Fable read those strips blind and agreed on 32, and 7 became corrections. A second, taller strip of the 1898 printing settled 42 of 66 differences that the first strip had left unclear.
- **Proofreading:** four reviewers read all 574 rule-drafted corrections and reported 80. Twenty-seven of the reports are single words where the 1913 typists' text agrees with the record and both readings make sense. Under the ruling of 10 November 2026 those follow the print, and the ledger lists them.

## Corrections

- **575 follow the ruling of 10 November 2026.** The record's reading came from the typists. Examples: Infusive "spring" for "spirit", Inform "shape" for "share", Jobation "long" for "hand", Indistinct "for" for "four", Landlouper "motley" for "moltey", and Juvia "Bertholletia". Indoctrinate regains "instruct in, or imbue with, principles or doctrines", Intellect "sometimes, the capacity for higher forms of knowledge", Jig "in a rhythm of triplets, commonly six-eighths", and Infidel "especially, one who does not believe in the divine origin and authority of Christianity". Insidious regains its synonym list, Keen its note on compounds, and Landmark, Justice, Instill and Kine a printed quotation each.
- **223 undo a later GCIDE change.** Kotow and Leucocythemia regain the printed definitions that GCIDE had replaced with "Same as kowtow" and "Same as leukemia". Others: Katabolic "as, katabolic processes, which give rise to substances (katastates) of decreasing complexity", Indophenol "has not yet been isolated", and "Want of" for "Lack of" in Indiscrimination, Inopportunity, Insubmission and Irreconciliation.
- **Kit, Jape and Lantern** regain printed text the record lacked: the verb Kit and the noun Kit "A kitten", the noun Jape, and the phrase "Lantern fly, Lantern carrier". Join regains "To join battle, To join issue."
- **Inflected forms:** 21 are corrected, among them Inlay "Inlaid", Innuendo "Innuendoes", Inswathe "Inswathing", Intaglio "Intaglios", and the Latin plurals "Infulæ" and "Injuriæ".
- **Infinitive** carried the printed entry Infinitively, and Iterate carried Iterately. Each is now its own record.

## Kept and held

- **The print is wrong:** Karyomiton "kytomyton", King "briliantly", Kritarchy "Jepthhah", Intoxicate "excesssively" and Levogyrate "cr".
- **Probable 1913 revisions:** Irrefrangibility's "irrefrangibleness" and the usage marks of Irreptitious, Jaw and Laystall stand as the 1913 typists' text has them.
- **Held for the person (54 questions):**
  - **Three printed headings the record lacks or spells otherwise.** The print's Isochrous, which the record has as Isochroous. The print's Inosite, which survives only as Inositol. The printed cross-reference "Jaganath, Jaganatha", which a later editor folded into Juggernaut.
  - **Lectern and Lecturn,** whose entries the record has exchanged, and **Kowtow and Kilter,** which the print sets as cross-references to Kotow and Kelter.
  - **Forty-seven passages that GCIDE rewrote,** where restoring the printed wording removes text the later-additions register lists or would blend two wordings: -ine, Index, Infidel, Inflation, Inning, Insulated, Interest, Intromission, Iridium, Islamism, Isomeric, Itchy, Jacobian, Jaguar, Jainism, Jargon, Joule, Journalism, Journeyman, Junior, Jupiter, Jury, Kephalin, Kind, Kindergarten, Kindling, Kit, Kitchen, Kiwikiwi, Knuckle, Kurd, Labarraque's solution, Lace, Lactose, Lagomorpha, Lamellibranchiata, Lamp, Lanthanum, Last, Lattice, Laughing, Lavender, Leitmotiv, Lend, Lerot, Liaison and Libyan.
  - Each is in the review queue with crops of both printings. Joule's question notes that the 1898 printing has an older definition, so only the 1907 crop matches.
- **Held for a later pass (2):** Item and Itinerary hold their homographs in another order than the print's.

## Limits

- Pronunciation marks, punctuation, capitals and italics are not compared.
- A single word that the 1913 printing changed cannot be told from a typist's slip without the 1913 page. The ruling of 10 November 2026 treats it as a slip.
- An entry that runs over from page 750 or onto page 851 is compared only as far as this batch's pages.
- The 1913 printing itself was not read. The only scan found is access-restricted.
