# Page-reading batch 11

Two models read printed pages 851 to 960 without seeing the record, and their agreed reading is compared with the record word by word. These pages end volume 1. This batch makes 751 corrections in 607 entries, adds 4 entries that the electronic text never had, and gives 8 records their printed spelling. It opens 55 holds: 49 questions in the review queue and 6 entries whose homographs stand in another order than the print's. The batch grants no complete-entry review credit.

The method is the one [batch 2](dictionary-audit-page-reading-2.md) describes, with the changes of batches [3](dictionary-audit-page-reading-3.md) to [10](dictionary-audit-page-reading-10.md). Nothing in the method changed for this batch.

## Pages and readers

- **Pages:** leaves n956 to n1065 of the 1907 volume 1 scan, Lift through Mythological, about 268,000 words in 7,909 printed entries.
- **Readers:** Opus and Sonnet. Opus alone found 53 differences the pair had missed, Fable read those strips blind and agreed on 30, and 5 became corrections. A second, taller strip of the 1898 printing settled 43 of 56 differences that the first strip had left unclear.
- **Proofreading:** four reviewers read all 530 rule-drafted corrections and reported 92. Fifty-five of the reports are single words where the 1913 typists' text agrees with the record and both readings make sense. Under the ruling of 10 November 2026 those follow the print, and the ledger lists them. Seventeen reports found that a correction would mix the printed wording with a later rewrite, and those passages went to the person.

## A check of the method

Fable read pages 751 to 784 blind as a third reader after [batch 10](dictionary-audit-page-reading-10.md) had corrected them. Its reading differs from the corrected record in 30 places. Twenty-six are differences batch 10 had already recorded and left, most of them held for the person. The other four are comparison noise at the entries In- and -in. The third reading found no further correction and contradicted none of the corrections applied on those pages, so a third reader is not added to the method.

## Corrections

- **613 follow the ruling of 10 November 2026.** The record's reading came from the typists. Examples: Mona and Mouse "African" for "American", Loiter "loose" for "louse", Marl "making" for "marking", Modest "restrained" for "restraining", Lunule "(Geom.)" for "(Chem.)", and Montanist "Montanus". Lost regains its sense 3, "Not gained or won; as, a lost prize; a lost battle." Make-up regains "often, the way in which an actor is dressed, painted, etc., in personating a character", Luncheon "an informal or light repast, as between breakfast and dinner", and Madder four of its five colour names.
- **138 undo a later GCIDE change.** Metric regains the printed account of the metric system where GCIDE had left "See metric system". Others: Mormon "One of a sect", Monetization "adopting as money", Moonshee "Mohammedan", Manatee "Hayti", and Lutein "yelk".
- **Ludicrous, Mail, Maltese, Man-of-war and Maidenhair** regain printed text the record lacked: Ludicrous's synonym paragraph, and the phrases "Mail bag", "Mail boat", "Maltese cat", "Man-of-war bird", "Maidenhair grass" and "Maidenhair tree".
- **Inflected forms:** 10 are corrected, among them Locality "Localities", Lucubrate "Lucubrating", Margin "Margining", Mince "Mincing" and Monsignore "Monsignori".
- **Three citations** had the letter I for the numeral: "1 Kings" under Light, "1 Sam." under Little and "1 Cor." under Man.
- **Mortar** carried the printed entry Morter under its own headword, and **Mulada** carried Muir. Each is now its own record. Mexical and Molendinarious had a misspelled first headword.

## Limits

- Pronunciation marks, punctuation, capitals and italics are not compared.
- A single word that the 1913 printing changed cannot be told from a typist's slip without the 1913 page. The ruling of 10 November 2026 treats it as a slip.
- An entry that runs over from page 850 is compared only as far as this batch's pages.
- The 1913 printing itself was not read. The only scan found is access-restricted.
